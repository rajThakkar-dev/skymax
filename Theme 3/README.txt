SKYMAX SOLUTIONS — THEME 3 / EDITORIAL PRECISION
=================================================

A complete, responsive, seven-page static website concept for Skymax Solutions.
Open index.html directly in a browser. There is no build process and no external dependency.

DESIGN DIRECTION
- Warm editorial canvas with navy, electric blue and teal brand accents.
- Blueprint/grid details communicate structure, transformation and delivery discipline.
- The three offset delivery panels echo the three bands in the supplied Skymax logo.
- Theme 2 logo, favicon, touch icon and bundled font assets are reused as requested.
- Motion respects the user's reduced-motion preference.

PAGES
- index.html — Homepage
- workday.html — Workday consulting
- services.html — Enterprise technology services
- about.html — Company profile
- careers.html — Talent and careers
- contact.html — Contact and preview enquiry form
- privacy.html — Preview privacy notice

ASSETS
- assets/foundation.css — Shared structural foundation from Theme 2
- assets/styles.css — Theme 3 visual direction and responsive refinements
- assets/site.js — Navigation, reveal effects and safe demo-form behaviour
- assets/logo-*.png — Supplied Theme 2 logo suite
- assets/fonts/ — Bundled Montserrat and Inter files
